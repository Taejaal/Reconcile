⚠

Known limitations

  • The matcher does not see a merchant-category or account-ID field, so structural decoys (same amount, same date, different counterparty) cannot currently be told apart from a true match.
  • Greedy assignment is used instead of optimal (Hungarian) assignment, which accounts for 5 of the 14 false positives observed on the stress-test dataset.
  • One-to-many splits (one bank deposit covering multiple ledger invoices) are not handled by the pairwise matching architecture.
  • Text similarity is not currency/locale-aware and does not normalize merchant name variants or abbreviations beyond prefix/suffix stripping.
